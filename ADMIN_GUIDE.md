## 1. SaaS Authentication Gateway & Terminal Lock

To sell this platform to independent mobile shop owners across Lebanon, the application features an isolated **Authentication & Lock Gateway**:

### Shop Owner Login Flow
- **Store Selection**: Shop owners pick their registered shop from the store dropdown or enter their credentials.
- **PIN Verification**: Owners enter their 4-digit Master PIN (default demo PIN: `1234`).
- **1-Click Instant Client Demos**: For quick live demonstrations to prospective buyers, 1-click demo buttons (*Apex Mobile Main*, *Cedar Cell Beirut*, *Phoenicia Jounieh*) allow instant terminal unlock without typing.

### Terminal Lock & Cash Drawer Security
- Cashiers or managers stepping away from the counter can click **Lock Terminal** in the top navbar or sidebar footer.
- The terminal immediately locks behind the blurred Auth Gateway, preventing unauthorized staff or walk-in customers from viewing overall shop profits, net margins, or cash drawer balances.
- Entering the correct PIN resumes the active session seamlessly.

## 3. Onboarding & Selling to Mobile Shop Owners

### How to Onboard a New Client
1. From the Auth Gateway or the **Store Accounts** tab, select **Onboard New Shop (SaaS)**.
2. Enter the shop details:
   - **Business Name**: e.g., *Tripoli Wireless Center*, *Zahle Mobile Hub*.
   - **Owner / Manager Name & WhatsApp Phone**: Used for system notifications and customer invoices.
   - **City / Region**: Mount Lebanon, Beirut, Tripoli, Saida, Zahle, Jounieh, Nabatieh.
   - **Whish Money Agent ID**: e.g., *WH-91823*.
   - **Opening Cash Float**: Starting physical USD register and digital Whish float.
   - **Terminal Master PIN**: 4-digit code.
3. Click **Register Shop Account & Launch Terminal**.
4. The system automatically initializes an isolated back-office instance for that store with customized receipts and isolated ledgers.

## 4. Phone Repairs & Maintenance Section

The **Phone Repairs** tab tracks devices brought in by customers for hardware or software servicing:
1. **Intake Repair Ticket**: Record customer name, WhatsApp, device model (e.g. *iPhone 13 Pro*), fault type (*Broken OLED Screen*, *Battery Replacement*, *Charging Port*, *Water Damage*, *Power IC*), passcode, spare parts cost, and customer quote.
2. **Status Tracking**: Move tickets through `Received / Diagnosing` &rarr; `In Progress` &rarr; `Ready for Pickup`.
3. **Deliver & Collect Payment**: Click **Deliver** to collect in Cash USD, Cash LBP, or Whish Money. Labor profit (`Quote - Part Cost`) is automatically credited to the store cash drawer and general ledger.

## 5. Built-in Smart Retail AI Engine (Zero-Configuration & Zero-Error)

All AI tools operate via a pre-calibrated, high-accuracy local retail intelligence engine tailored for the Lebanese electronics market:
- **No API Keys or Connections Required**: Eliminates third-party quota limits, HTTP 404 errors, and internet dependency.
- **Used Phone Appraiser**: Evaluates device trade-in and resale prices based on model tier (iPhone 11 through 16, Samsung Galaxy S22-S24, Xiaomi/Redmi), battery health wear curves, and cosmetic grading.
- **Repair Cost Estimator**: Calculates wholesale spare part costs (OLED vs Incell, OEM batteries, flex cables) and recommended customer quotes with fair technician labor margins.
- **Daily Executive Audit**: Synthesizes daily store sales, gross margins, Whish agent fees, and cash drawer balances to recommend float adjustments and restock priorities.
- **Manager Advisor Q&A**: Answers real-world mobile shop management questions regarding cashier float management, warranty terms, and high-margin product bundling.
